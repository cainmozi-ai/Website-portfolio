## Before you deploy

1. **Netlify Forms.** Site configuration > Forms > Enable form detection, then deploy
   and send yourself a test message. Until then the contact form returns a 404. The
   email link above the form works regardless.
2. **Review the branch on a deploy preview**, not straight to production. Check: share
   image appears in LinkedIn Post Inspector, `/work/corvette-midnight-run` redirects,
   no broken images on N.O.R.A. or Midnight Run.
