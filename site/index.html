BYŌDŌ-IN PHOENIX HALL — JAPANESE VR ENVIRONMENT

Environment Art · Blender · Unreal Engine 5 · VR 360°

A faithful reconstruction of the Byōdō-in Phoenix Hall as a static VR environment, part of the 'Echoes of the Past' university project exploring historical spaces as interactive museum exhibits. BATTLE OF BRITAIN — RAF SCRAMBLE SEQUENCE

Cinematic Animation · Blender · Unreal Engine 5 · Historical Research

A cinematic short recreating an RAF scramble during the 1940 Battle of Britain, centred on a Spitfire Mk IX at RAF Kenley. Part of the 'Echoes of the Past' project — hard-surface modelling from mechanical blueprints, atmospheric volumetrics, and UE5 Sequencer animation. Inspired by Julien Herbey's approach to history-driven 3D narrative.

ABOUT

Ex Labore Venustas

From Work, Beauty

I'm a 3D artist working primarily in Blender, expanding into Unreal Engine 5. I specialise in atmospheric rendering, environment art, and cinematic storytelling — work that sits at the intersection of gothic precision and historical narrative. I also work with tactile processes: hand-carved lino printing, embroidery, and 3D printing. Craft isn't limited to a screen.

Currently: Second-year Graphic Design student at the University of Salford, specialising in 3D. Available for studio placements, contract work, and collaborative projects.
