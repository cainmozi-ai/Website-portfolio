BYŌDŌ-IN PHOENIX HALL

Environment Art · Blender · Unreal Engine 5 · VR 360°

University project exploring how historical spaces can function as interactive museum exhibits.

A faithful reconstruction of the Byōdō-in Phoenix Hall as a static VR environment, part of the 'Echoes of the Past' project. Modelled in Blender from Machiya architectural reference and Heian-period design language, textured in Substance Painter, and rendered as 8K 360° panoramas in Unreal Engine 5. Accuracy to the historical record was non-negotiable.

EX LABORE VENUSTAS

From Work, Beauty

I'm a 3D artist working primarily in Blender, expanding into Unreal Engine 5. I specialise in atmospheric rendering, environment art, and cinematic storytelling — work that sits at the intersection of gothic precision and historical narrative. I also work with tactile processes: hand-carved lino printing, embroidery, and 3D printing. Craft isn't limited to a screen.

Every project begins with a question: would this hold up in 1425 and in 2025? That dual-era test shapes everything — respecting the discipline of what came before while pushing technical boundaries forward. Design as ritual, not production. Refinement over agreement. The work earns its place through labour, not decoration.
